# Credit Dragon video script and storyboard

| Time | Speaker | Visual | Draft narration and action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Naomi | ASIC statistic, then Mia’s payday calendar and overlapping bills | “In ASIC’s 2020 report, one in five buy now, pay later users surveyed said they’d missed or paid another bill late to keep up with their BNPL payments. Meet Mia. She’s 21, studies full-time and works part-time. Her pay lands fortnightly, but her bills are spread across the month. After a late card repayment, she wants to know what to do next.” |
| 1:00–2:00 | Santiago | Credit Dragon title and phone home screen | “Credit Dragon turns transaction patterns into a short plan Mia can act on. Westpac could use its own transaction history, with Mia’s permission, to explain why each question is relevant.” |
| 2:00–6:30 | Shawn | Scan → question → plan → compare → approve → dragon → AR | “Mia starts a scan using sample data. The app notices her phone bill and asks whether she’d like to compare plans. She can see why or skip. Her plan includes a repayment reminder and a cheaper phone plan. She reviews a simulated offer, approves it in the demo, and earns dragon XP. Now she can place her dragon on the table.” |
| 6:30–7:30 | Carlos | Terminal recording with fictional input and generated output | “Here is the script reading Mia’s sample transactions and generating a question and its explanation. The app uses the saved output for this demo.” |
| 7:30–9:00 | Santiago and Naomi | Consent, explanation and approval screens | “Lower bills can leave Mia more room to cover repayments. In the prototype, savings are illustrative and XP records progress on her plan. A pilot would test whether customers understand the recommendations and follow through on repayment goals. Account access would be opt-in and any plan switch would require approval.” |
| 9:00–9:40 | All | Team on camera, then final AR shot | “Credit Dragon gives Mia one clear next step and a character that grows as she follows through.” |

Record the main journey, AI terminal and AR moment separately. Keep the approved offer and simulated savings visible long enough to read. Capture the app’s actual state before recording the final voiceover and adjust the narration to match.

Opening statistic source: [ASIC Report 672, p. 16](https://download.asic.gov.au/media/5852803/rep672-published-16-november-2020-2.pdf#page=16).
