Stage Reference & Regulatory Specification

  1. Stage 1 · Client Layer

    Anchor Goal Selection & Cryptographic App Attest

    Customer sets their financial quest (Home Loan, Car Finance, Clean CCR Slate) in the Westpac app. Requests are hardware-signed to guarantee client authenticity.

    • Built natively using Westpac GEL Design System tokens (#DA1710 Westpac Red, #1F1C4F Westpac Navy) for seamless integration inside the Westpac Mobile Banking App.
    • Customer selects an active Anchor Goal (goals.json), which calibrates downstream credit simulations (e.g., prioritizing on-time CCR streaks for Mia, or APRA 3% credit card limit reduction for David).
    • Apple's Secure Enclave generates an Apple App Attest cryptographic assertion over TLS 1.3, blocking spoofed clients or automated bots from querying the AI gateway.
    • Captures explicit customer opt-in under Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Part IIIA) before accessing transaction or credit heuristics.
  2. Stage 2 · Perimeter & Privacy

    Zero-Trust Perimeter & PII Isolation Shield (APRA CPS 234)

    Inbound requests traverse AWS WAF into an isolated privacy subnet where all personal identifiers and social demographics are stripped prior to AI processing.

    • Hosted strictly within the AWS Sydney Region (ap-southeast-2) to uphold Australian financial data sovereignty requirements.
    • Complies with APRA Prudential Standard CPS 234 (Information Security): an AWS Lambda PII Scrubber replaces customer names, BSBs, and account numbers with synthetic session UUIDs (e.g., usr_99481a).
    • Demographic Neutrality Guardrail: Age, visa status, nationality, and passport data are stripped at the perimeter, ensuring 100% compliance with the Australian Credit Reporting Privacy Code.
  3. Stage 3 · Core Banking & Heuristics

    Deterministic Pre-Processor, National Medians & Payday Bill-Shield

    A deterministic Python engine analyzes 90 days of core ledger transactions in under 5ms, comparing recurring bills against national medians and detecting payday timing collisions.

    • Ingests read-only transaction history from Amazon RDS (plus external accounts and BNPL lines if connected via accredited Consumer Data Right / Open Banking feeds).
    • Unbranded Median Benchmark Engine (benchmarks.json): Compares recurring bills against national consumer medians (ACCC / ABS data). For example, flags Mia's $89/mo mobile plan against the $45/mo Australian median to surface a $44/mo ($528/yr) saving opportunity.
    • Payday Bill-Shield: Identifies direct debits scheduled within 48 hours *prior* to fortnightly salary credits (e.g., Mia's Thursday gym debit bouncing 24h before her Friday payday, costing $19 in combined dishonour fees) and prepares a PayTo friday realignment.
    • Punctuality Buffer Sweep: Models an automated $22/fortnight micro-sweep from bill savings into a dedicated Westpac Life protection stash so minimum card repayments never bounce.
  4. Stage 4 · Regulated AI Sandbox

    Zero Data Retention Reasoning & Debt Reallocation Vector

    An enterprise LLM inside Westpac's private VPC synthesizes the pre-processed facts into empathetic questions and actionable debt reallocation plans.

    • Invokes enterprise Gemini 2.5 Flash / Amazon Bedrock via the Portkey AI Gateway with Zero Data Retention (ZDR) enforced—customer data is never retained or used to train public models.
    • Competitor Brand Suppression: System prompt guardrails prohibit naming external competitors; savings recommendations are framed neutrally as an "Optimized Market-Rate Tier-1 Plan".
    • Debt Reallocation Vector: Explicitly links bill savings to credit score mechanics: "Spending less does not directly raise your Equifax score, but routing your $44/mo telco saving into your Westpac Flex Card clears revolving debt 4 months faster and projects a +35 pt CCR improvement."
    • Validates output against a strict JSON schema (questions.json and insights.json) requiring exact related_transaction_ids citations, a "why_asking" trace, and a "Skip question" option, then caches to Amazon DynamoDB for sub-50ms mobile hydration.
  5. Stage 5 · Execution & Gamification

    Human-in-the-Loop Approval (ASIC RG 209) & AR Dragon Evolution

    The customer answers context cards and manually approves bill switches or debit shifts, earning XP and Coins that evolve their 3D Westpac Dragon in Augmented Reality.

    • Complies with ASIC Regulatory Guide 209 & 248 and Australia's Voluntary AI Safety Standard: the AI never executes autonomous debits or unilateral plan switches. Every action requires an explicit customer "Approve" tap.
    • Approving a bill switch, activating the Punctuality Buffer Sweep, or maintaining an on-time card repayment streak awards XP and Coins defined in Santiago's rewards.json engine.
    • The iOS app's native ARKit + RealityKit engine renders the Westpac Dragon on the user's table, playing level-up particle animations (Egg ➔ Baby ➔ Teen ➔ Adult) and unlocking Westpac Life bonus savings interest perks.
